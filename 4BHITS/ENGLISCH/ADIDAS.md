# Adidas: "Impossible Is Nothing" — AIDA Analysis

![Adidas - Impossible Is Nothing](adidas.jpg)

![](adidas2.jpg)

### The Campaign

* **Brand:** Adidas
* **Slogan:** "Impossible Is Nothing"
* **Core Idea:** Conquering doubt through grit, resilience, and self-belief.

### 1. Attention

- **High-contrast visuals:** Gritty, black-and-white portrait of Muhammad Ali.   
- **Bold typography:** Prominent, minimalist slogan placement.   
- **Iconic badge:** Instantly recognizable Adidas Trefoil logo[cite: 11].
- *Captures focus instantly without visual clutter.*

### 2. Interest

- **Human struggle:** Centers on real adversity rather than effortless victory.
- **Emotional resonance:** Encourages viewers to see their own battles in the athlete's story.
- **Cultural crossover:** Appeals equally to sports performance and streetwear culture.
- *Maintains curiosity through raw, relatable vulnerability.*

### 3. Desire

- **Mindset shift:** Associates Adidas with overcoming limitations and defying odds.
- **Identity appeal:** Wearing the brand signals resilience, authenticity, and confidence.
- **Lifestyle integration:** Transcends sportswear to represent personal triumph.
- *Generates an emotional need to adopt the brand's fearless attitude.*

### 4. Action

- **Direct imperative:** "Impossible Is Nothing" challenges the audience to break their own boundaries.
- **Active conversion:** Prompts consumers to train, join adiClub events, and explore product lines.
- **Clear takeaway:** Serves as a decisive push to take the next step.
